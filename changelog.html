Teams++ Changelog

What's new and improved in Teams++

  • v0.3.0

    October 2026 Current
    • NEWInbox Zero: Archive any post to hide it, just like read email. A new reply brings the thread straight back, and clearing the channel shows a confetti celebration.
    • NEWPinned post bars: Pinned posts appear in thin purple bars at the top of the channel. Click a bar to jump to the post, or unpin it from the bar.
    • NEWPin any post: A pin button on every post adds it to the top of the channel in a lighter violet, below posts pinned by a channel moderator.
    • NEWOptional access: Teams++ stays completely off until you click Enable on Microsoft Teams. Nothing is injected before that, and access can be revoked in Chrome at any time.
    • IMPROVESynced everywhere: Your archived posts, pins and inbox view are stored in Chrome Sync so they follow you to every Chrome profile.
    • REMOVEDFocused feature set: Removed the unread banner, date dividers, avatar spacing, unread line, floating compose box and pinned shortcut so Teams++ can do Inbox Zero and pins properly.

  • v0.2.0

    October 2026
    • NEWTeams++ branding: Renamed from Better Teams to Teams++, with a purple-and-white look that matches Microsoft Teams.
    • NEWNew popup: One card per tweak, with switches, tooltips, and links to the welcome screen and changelog.
    • NEWWelcome screen: A short tour on first install that explains every tweak.
    • NEWChangelog: This timeline, also opened automatically after an update.
    • IMPROVESix channel tweaks: Unread banner, sticky date dividers, separated posts, red unread line, floating compose box, and the pinned messages shortcut.
    • IMPROVESettings sync: Every switch is stored in Chrome Sync and applies to open Teams tabs immediately.

  • v0.1.0

    October 2026
    • NEWFirst release: The original six channel tweaks — unread banner, sticky date dividers, separated posts, red unread line, floating compose box, and the pinned messages shortcut.
    • IMPROVEWorks on Teams web: Supports teams.microsoft.com, teams.cloud.microsoft, and teams.live.com.
