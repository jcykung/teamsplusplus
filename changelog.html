Teams++ Changelog

What's new and improved in Teams++

  • v1.1.1

    November 2026 Current
    • FIXA read mark no longer goes missing on its way to the other browsers: two copies of a channel open at once could end up disagreeing for good — a post read on the laptop still sitting Unread on the desktop, with nothing left to put it right. The marks are merged per post, so the fault was never in the comparing but in what had been stored: a page still loading put back the map it had read before a change from the other browser arrived, and a write Chrome refused — the item over its quota, or the minute's allowance used up — was dropped without a word. The load now folds the two maps together instead of choosing one, and a refused write is offered again a little later, so a mark reaches the other browser even when that browser was shut at the time.
    • IMPROVEWho syncs with whom is said plainly: the README no longer leaves it to be guessed. Reads follow the browser's own sync — Chrome with Chrome, signed in to the same account — and they do not cross to a different browser, such as Brave or Dia, which keep their own marks.

  • v1.1.0

    November 2026
    • NEWA post you have read can come back, without looking new: a card you have opened that is then answered or reacted to wears the mark again, and this time it says Updated. The tooltip names what arrived while it was folded — "2 new replies and 1 new reaction since you read it" — so the mark is the answer to "what did I miss here?" rather than a fresh arrival. Opening the card clears it as before, and a post that has gained nothing stays folded and read.
    • IMPROVETeams' own Mark as unread is left to Teams: the menu item still badges that post, and the newer posts above it, in Teams' own list. The cards do not change: a post read here stays read, because a mark put back that way carries no reply and no reaction behind it. Only the post itself moving on reopens one, which is what makes an Updated card different from an Unread one. Reads also reach the other browsers signed in to the same profile, so a card opened here is read there too.
    • FIXUpdated is never dressed up as Unread: a card opened while Teams was still drawing the row could record the reply count as unreadable, and that count appearing a moment later looked like a reply had arrived — nothing had, so there was nothing to name, and the card came back saying Unread with a purple border. That is what a manual mark on a post three down from the top handed to the top post. A count now only counts when both ends of the comparison read a number, the card's own time label no longer counts as activity at all, and a mark brought back by real activity always says Updated, naming what it gained or saying plainly that there is new activity.

  • v1.0.7

    November 2026
    • FIXThe date belongs to the post you are reading: scrolling through one long open post walked the held date through the days of the posts further down the channel — Tuesday, October 20, then September 21, then August 31 — naming posts that were nowhere near the reader, over the one that had not moved. The date is now only ever the day of the post under the edge: that post's own line when the line has reached the edge, and otherwise the day that post belongs to. A line belonging to any other post is not consulted, so scrolling inside a post cannot change what it says.
    • FIXThe list order is read from the whole channel, not its two ends: Teams positions and recycles these rows itself, so a single row holding the stamp of the post it showed before — or one waiting below the fold — was enough to turn the list round. That drew every day's line on the wrong side of its gap and moved the held date to the far edge, where it named the days arriving there instead of the day being read. The order is now read from the posts where they are drawn, one neighbour against the next, and the majority decides; the day's lines are drawn again the moment it changes.
    • FIXA post keeps the day its newest reply gave it: Teams re-renders a row as it is scrolled into view, and can leave the reply out for a moment, which made the post fall back to the day it was written — moving the post, and every date line under it, while the reader was in the middle of it. The newest activity a post has shown is now remembered, so its day does not slip back to an older one.
    • IMPROVETwo switches, and they work apart: Compact posts and Date lines are now independent — the day's lines can be kept with Teams' own posts as well as with the cards. The Manually mark as read switch is gone, because the rule it guarded is now the only one: a card keeps its Unread badge until you open it, and Teams clearing its own badge behind the scenes does not count as reading it.

  • v1.0.6

    November 2026
    • FIXThe date line stays in the gap: a post that grew while you were in it — a reply box opened, a thread or an image arrived — pushed the posts below it down but left the day's line where the gap used to be, so the line was drawn across the post. The line is placed again whenever a post changes height, so it always sits in the space between the posts rather than on top of or behind one.
    • FIXClosing a post brings it back to the top: opening a card scrolled it up so you read it from its first line; closing it — by clicking it or pressing Escape — left the condensed card wherever the cursor and the shrinking page put it. The card now comes back to the top too, so the list carries on from the post you were just reading.
    • IMPROVEDate lines are a switch: the popup has a new Date lines switch, on by default, which turns the day lines between the posts — and the date held at the edge you read from — on or off. With it off, the cards are drawn with nothing between the days.

  • v1.0.5

    November 2026
    • IMPROVEOne word for unread: the New badge is gone — a post that has arrived, or one answered since you last read it, simply wears an Unread badge and a purple outline until you open it. The Last read line and its copy are gone with it.
    • IMPROVEDates where the reading starts: in the default view the day's line sits above the first post of the day, so each run starts with its date; with new posts at the bottom it closes the day above it. Whichever edge you read from, the date holds there until the next day's line takes over. The held line is as wide as the posts and its date centred on them. Where the pane leaves an empty strip above the list it is drawn in that, so the posts never pass under it; where it does hold over the list, it stands on the colour of the cards behind it, so what scrolls beneath cannot be read through it.
    • IMPROVENo more Ctrl+F nag: Teams' black "press Ctrl+F to find in this channel" nudge, which it drops over the list when you start scrolling, is hidden.
    • FIXSteadier scrolling: the list no longer changes height under the reader, so scrolling — especially with new posts at the bottom — stops jumping. The order of the list is read once and kept, instead of being re-guessed from posts that are mid-render.
    • FIXPosts arrive folded: a post Teams mounted was laid out at its full height and only turned into a card a moment later, so the list grew by a whole post and gave the room back. Teams keeps the scroll steady by measuring exactly that change and correcting for it, and with new posts at the bottom — at the edge you read from — the correction is what made the posts skip a few at a time. The card is now on the row before the browser lays it out, so there is nothing to correct for, and a row Teams re-renders keeps its card rather than emptying and refilling.
    • FIXA post you mark unread is the only one marked: Teams also marks the posts around the one you chose as unread, and those marks are the channel's, so they were still there after a reload — the cards came back as Unread in a block. The compact view now remembers how far the channel had got when you made the mark, and holds everything stamped at or before that moment read. Your post keeps its Unread badge and purple outline until you open it; the rest stay read, whatever Teams badges on them, and a post that arrives afterwards still says Unread.

  • v1.0.4

    November 2026
    • FIXCopying text no longer closes the post: Highlighting words in an open post — dragging over them, or double-clicking one — used to fold the post, because the click that ends a selection looks like the click that closes it. Closing now waits a moment and stands aside for a selection, so the post stays open while you copy.
    • IMPROVEOne post open at a time: Opening a card closes the post you were reading, so moving down a channel leaves one post open rather than a trail of them.
    • IMPROVEAn opened post scrolls to the top: The post comes up to the top of the list as it opens, so you read it from its first line instead of wherever its card happened to sit.

  • v1.0.3

    November 2026
    • IMPROVEMarking a post unread is immediate: A post you mark unread now comes back as Unread with its purple border straight away, instead of waiting for Teams' own badge to change. The menu press is read the moment you make it, and the badge Teams puts back on the post you have open is taken as the same mark.
    • FIXOpening a card stays read: Opening a card clears its badge and border, and they stay cleared — Teams drawing its own badge again as it re-renders the row no longer brings the mark back.
    • FIXOnly the post you marked comes back: Teams also marks every message newer than the one you mark unread; those cards stay read here, wherever the mark was made from.
    • IMPROVEReady for the Chrome Web Store: The welcome screen, README and a published privacy policy now state plainly that Teams++ collects nothing, makes no network requests and runs only after you grant Teams access.

  • v1.0.2

    November 2026
    • IMPROVENew while Teams says new, then Unread: A folded card says New for as long as Teams still marks the post new. When Teams clears that badge the card says Unread in its place and keeps its purple border, so the post still waits for you to open it — reading it somewhere else in Teams no longer hides that it has not been read here.
    • IMPROVEManually mark as read is a switch: The popup has a new Manually mark as read switch, on by default, which turns the click-to-read rule on or off. With it off, a folded card follows Teams: the New badge and purple border show while Teams marks the post new and clear when Teams marks it read.
    • IMPROVEA line and a date between each day's posts: The posts are split by day with a light purple line carrying the date — Today, Yesterday, then the day's name — drawn where Teams' own Last read line goes, so a day's posts read as one run. A post lifted back up by a reply is filed under the day it came back, not the day it was written.
    • FIXUnread marks stay put: A post you have opened no longer comes back as New because Teams left its own badge in the row, or put one back on every message newer than one marked unread.
    • FIXOne post at a time: Marking a post unread no longer marks every message newer than it unread here: those cards stay read, and only the one you marked comes back, saying Unread straight away with its purple border, until you open it again.
    • FIXPosts fold when you change channel: Whatever you had opened in one channel is condensed again, so arriving in the next channel — or coming back to one you left — always shows it as a list of cards.
    • FIXMarking a post unread sticks: Choosing Teams' own Mark as unread used to be undone the moment you opened the card to reach the menu. A post marked unread now keeps its New badge and purple border until you open its card again.
    • FIXMarking a read post unread brings the badge back: Teams' own Mark as unread is now read as it is clicked, so a post that was already read returns to New with the purple outline instead of being mistaken for the badge left over from opening it.

  • v1.0.1

    November 2026
    • IMPROVENew stays new until you open it: Opening a condensed card is now what marks a post read. A post Teams marks New keeps its badge and purple border until you click it open — Teams' badge clearing when the channel opens no longer counts as reading it — and a reply, reaction, attachment or edit brings it back.

  • v1.0

    November 2026
    • NEWCompact posts, ready for prime time: The first stable release. Every channel post is drawn as a compact card — author, time, headline and two lines — and opens to the full post, replies and all, with a click or Escape.
    • FIXNew and Last read are back on the cards: A folded post shows Teams' New badge beside the time again, and the Last read divider is copied from Teams' own and centred in the gap under the last post you read — same style, same position and the same spacing as before Inbox Zero took it over.
    • IMPROVENew posts stand out: A condensed card Teams marks New now has a bright purple border instead of the usual hairline, and the channel's name in the left rail turns the same purple while Teams bolds it for unread activity — so what has arrived is distinct from what you have read even before the badge is read.
    • IMPROVEInbox Zero is taking a break: The Inbox/Archive feature and its confetti are switched off for this release while they get more work. They are still in the build, but no switch turns them on and they do not change the post list.

  • v0.6.6

    November 2026
    • REMOVEDPinned bars are gone: Teams does not put a channel's pinned list anywhere the extension can read without opening the In this channel pane, so the purple bars could only ever be empty or out of date. Inbox Zero, the Archive and Compact posts are unchanged.
    • IMPROVENew in Teams means Inbox in Teams++: A post Teams marks New now goes to the Inbox however old it is, and stays there until you archive it — the badge is written down the first time it is seen, so Teams clearing it on open no longer files the post behind your back. Archiving a post while the badge is still showing records that badge, so it does not bounce straight back; a later reply, reaction, attachment or edit still returns it to the Inbox.
    • IMPROVEArchived posts come back when they move on: A new reply, a reaction, a new attachment or an edit to a post you filed sends it straight back to the Inbox. Teams++ fingerprints the post when it is archived — its reply count, reactions, attachments and words — and compares that on every scan, so nothing new stays buried in the Archive.
    • FIXReplies are reachable again: Inbox Zero was treating the collapsed reply box under each post as a loading placeholder — Teams gives it a placeholder id — and hiding it took the Reply control away. Loading stand-ins are still hidden, but nothing you write in is.

  • v0.6.5

    November 2026
    • IMPROVEPins belong to one channel: A pin lives in the channel — and team — it was made in. Switching channels clears the bars and reads the new channel's pins afresh, so a post pinned in one Inbox never appears in another. The team is part of the channel's identity, so two channels with the same name in different teams stay separate too.
    • IMPROVEPinned bars are always on: The Pinned message bars switch is gone. Pinning is part of the Inbox rather than a preference, so an older saved "off" cannot leave a channel without its pins.
    • IMPROVENo more loading bar: The thin bar that crossed the top of the list while a view was loading is removed. Clicking Inbox or Archive still asks Teams for the Posts panel, and the posts follow as soon as Teams draws them.
    • FIXPurple bars keep their place: A bar no longer paints over Teams' own menus — the Add a tab dropdown and the rest now open above it.
    • FIXNothing follows you between tabs: An open post's attachments, images and media stay in the tab that owns it, and the Inbox Zero sign appears only in a channel Inbox that has nothing left to read.
    • IMPROVEThe finishing touches: Confetti bursts higher out of the Inbox Zero sign and fans out before it falls, and the Archive's unarchive button shows an archive box with an up arrow.

  • v0.6.4

    November 2026
    • NEWA bar for a view that is still loading: Clicking Inbox or Archive now answers at once. A thin bar crosses the top of the list while the posts for that view are being drawn, and leaves as soon as they are there — so coming back from another channel tab no longer looks like a blank page. A view that is still arriving is re-scanned every 200 ms, so its posts and their condensed cards appear as soon as Teams renders them.
    • FIXClicking a purple bar opens the post again: The hand-off to Teams' own Pinned list is gone. A bar now always opens its post in the channel — unfolded, focused, and lined up with the top of the scroll area — instead of doing nothing when that list was closed or off screen.
    • FIXRemoved pins lose their bars: A bar is kept only while Teams still lists the post as pinned or the post still carries Teams' pin marker; otherwise it is erased within a second or two. Old pins no longer leave purple bars behind.
    • FIXNo more floating Inbox/Archive strip: The small fallback pair that could appear by itself near the top of the page — in search results, an opened post, or a channel mid-switch — is gone. The tabs exist in Teams' own tab strip or not at all.

  • v0.6.3

    November 2026
    • IMPROVEPins are Teams' own: Only posts a channel moderator has pinned for everyone get a purple bar. The Pin button and the copy of each post kept with a pin are gone, so a bar never shows words Teams is not showing — and one post can no longer grow two bars, one with the message and one without it.
    • NEWA bar takes you to the post: Clicking a bar unfolds the post in the channel and lines its top up with the top of the scroll area, so it reads from its first line down, and the post takes the keyboard so Teams' shortcuts act on it. A pin Teams no longer lists has its bar removed rather than left behind for minutes afterwards.
    • IMPROVEA bar leads with the subject: A bar reads Author: subject and then the start of the message, instead of using the opening line of the body in place of the subject.
    • IMPROVEPinning stays in the Inbox: The Archive draws no bars and offers no pin, and a pinned post in the Archive keeps its Unarchive button, so a filed post can still be put back.
    • IMPROVEAn open post takes the keyboard: Opening a post hands it to Teams, so it is the message the keyboard shortcuts act on without a second click. Clicking it again folds it, and Escape does too. Status messages now appear in the middle of the posts rather than wherever the post happened to be.
    • IMPROVEFaster channel switches: The Inbox/Archive pair is drawn before the posts are measured, a half-drawn view asks for another pass instead of waiting for a click, and a channel switch no longer walks the whole mounted pane before the browser paints.

  • v0.6.2

    November 2026
    • FIXNo gaps between posts: A post the tab hides is now closed up and takes no room, so the Inbox and the Archive are each one unbroken run of posts in Teams' own order. The height it gave up is handed back as one constant block of room after the last post — and only while the tab still has a post on show. An emptied tab is left exactly as Teams laid it out, with the empty state in front of it, because collapsing the lot made Teams re-measure an empty pane on a loop.
    • FIXPosts arrive already closed: Older posts Teams fetched in the background used to paint at full height and collapse a moment later, so the list jumped for every batch — on first load, and whenever hovering made Teams render a few rows. They are now marked before the browser paints, so a batch is never seen arriving.
    • FIXThe Inbox stops paging the channel's history: The room now sits on the box Teams actually measures — the row container, three windows tall — instead of the scrolling element around it, which only produced a scrollbar while the posts still read as one row. A short Inbox no longer looks like the end of the channel.

  • v0.6.1

    November 2026
    • FIXNo more gaps between posts: A post the Inbox (or the Archive) hides used to keep its height, which left blank holes between the posts on show. Hidden posts are now closed up and take no room, so each tab is one unbroken run of posts in Teams' own order, starting at the top. The list keeps one constant block of room after its last post instead of a height measured from the hidden ones, so emptying the Inbox no longer sends Teams looking for the rest of the channel's history — and a tab with nothing left to show is left exactly as Teams laid it out, with the empty state in front of it, rather than collapsed to nothing.
    • FIXThe Inbox ends at its last post: Scrolling stops with the last post on screen at the bottom, and an Inbox shorter than the window does not scroll at all.
    • FIXPins stay in their channel: Pins are filed under the channel's own id, read from the full route rather than the path. New Teams keeps the channel in the hash, so every channel shared one path — and one bucket of pins — which is why a post pinned in one channel appeared in the next.
    • IMPROVEOne button on a pinned post: A pinned post keeps its Pin button and drops the Archive button, rather than showing an archive control that only says no. Unpin it and Archive is back.
    • IMPROVEPins you already made: Pins filed under the old shared key are adopted once by the channel you are in, so the bars you had are still there after the update.

  • v0.6.0

    November 2026
    • NEWRead and unread, not a private archive flag: The Inbox holds the posts you have not dealt with, and everything read is in the Archive. The moment you install Teams++ is stored once: anything posted after it starts in the Inbox, and everything already in the channel is read. No badge to race, no divider to misread.
    • IMPROVEA quieter card: Teams' New badge and its Last read line are gone from the compact view. With an Inbox and an Archive, the tabs already say what is read.
    • NEWOne button decides: Archiving marks a post read and files it in the Archive; Unarchive marks it unread and brings it back. Nothing else moves a post, so the two tabs always agree.
    • IMPROVEA first visit that makes sense: On install, the posts you have already read are in the Archive and the ones Teams shows as new are in the Inbox, with no tidying-up needed.
    • FIXAn Inbox that leaves the list alone: Filtered-out posts are hidden in place instead of being squashed to a hairline, so the Inbox no longer makes Teams page the channel's entire history into it the moment it opens. Older posts load when you scroll, as they should.

  • v0.5.1

    November 2026
    • NEWPost cards you can browse: Every post is a card — the author's picture, their name and the time, the post's opening line as a big bold headline, and the first two lines of the words under it. Hovering fills the card with Teams purple the way a button reads, and clicking it opens the post to its full height with replies, reactions and images; clicking again folds it back.
    • NEWNew and Last read, kept in the fold: A folded post carries Teams' own New badge, and the Last read divider closes the post you last read — under its card, in Teams' own style — the two markers that tell you where to start reading.
    • IMPROVEButtons for the post you are on: Archive and Pin no longer sit beside every post at once. They appear when you hover a post, and stay out while the post you opened is on screen, so the channel is quiet until you reach for something.
    • IMPROVELight and dark: The card follows Teams' own theme instead of borrowing its colour variables — white type-on-white is gone. It is a white card in light mode and a charcoal one in dark, and it repaints the moment Teams switches.
    • IMPROVEArchive is a filter: The Archive tab is the Posts list with only archived posts showing, so the violet edge and the blank ghost rows a filtered-out post left behind are gone.
    • IMPROVEArchiving you can watch: A post slides to the right and fades as it leaves the Inbox, so the eye sees where it went instead of finding it gone.
    • FIXNo stray buttons: An Archive/Pin pair no longer appears on its own in the middle of an empty Archive, and a filtered-out post leaves no controls behind.
    • FIXChannel posts only: Teams++ now runs in a channel's Posts area and nowhere else. Chats, Calendar, Files, Calls and the rest of Teams keep exactly the markup, styles and behaviour Teams gives them. The check asks the page itself — Teams' Posts tab, post rows and channel panes — instead of trusting the URL, which new Teams can leave bare while a channel is open.
    • REMOVEDOne switch, one place: The compact-posts toggle is out of Teams' tab strip; the switch in the popup is the one control for it.

The switch sits beside the Inbox and Archive tabs and in the popup, and stays in sync.
