Welcome to Teams++

By Jonathan Kung

Turn Microsoft Teams channels into an inbox. Archive posts when you are done with them, and keep every pinned post within easy reach.

Inbox Zero

Every post gets an Archive button. Archived posts disappear like read email, then come back automatically when someone replies. Clear the whole channel and you get confetti.

Pinned posts at the top

Pinned posts sit in a purple bar above the channel. Click one to open the post and its replies, and pin any post yourself with the pin button.

Synced everywhere

Your archive, your pins and your view are saved in Chrome Sync, so the same inbox follows you to every Chrome profile you sign in to.

Teams++ only runs on Microsoft Teams on the web. It reads the page you are already on and never sends anything anywhere.
